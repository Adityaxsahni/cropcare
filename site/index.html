Crop check · offline · Hindi and English

Check a crop leaf for disease from one photo, without internet

CropCare is an Android app for farmers in Bihar. Point the camera at a tomato, potato or maize leaf, and it suggests which of 14 common conditions the leaf shows and what to do next, in Hindi or English.

Status: in development. The app is not yet available to download. It will first be tested with a small group of farmers, after an agriculture expert has checked the treatment advice.

How it works

  1. Open the app and place one leaf inside the box on the camera screen.
  2. The app checks the photo on the phone itself. Nothing is uploaded, so it works in the field with no signal.
  3. You see the likely condition, how sure the app is, and simple steps to take. A button reads the steps aloud in Hindi.
  4. If the app is not sure, it says so and asks for a clearer photo instead of guessing.

The app is a first check, not a diagnosis. For serious cases it points farmers to their nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).

Potato (आलू)Early blight, late blight, healthy
Maize (मक्का)Common rust, northern leaf blight, gray leaf spot, healthy

Rice and wheat matter more in Bihar, but public photo sets of their diseases taken in real fields are still small. They are planned for a later version.

How we test it

Many crop disease models report very high accuracy on lab photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds, then do much worse on photos taken in a field. CropCare is measured on three separate sets of photos:

  • PlantVillage: lab photos, used for training.
  • PlantDoc: real-world photos from the internet, with its own test split.
  • Our own field photos: taken on farms near us and labelled by people who know the diseases. These are used only once, for the final test, and never for training.

Treatment advice

The treatment steps in the app are written in plain Hindi and English. Before the app reaches farmers, every step will be checked by an agriculture expert, and the expert’s name will be shown in the app. The app never gives pesticide doses from its own text; it asks farmers to follow the product label and their KVK.

Privacy

Photos never leave the phone. If a farmer agrees, the app can send anonymous results (the condition found, how sure it was, and whether the farmer said it was correct) when the phone is online. It never sends names, phone numbers, photos or locations. Read the privacy policy.
