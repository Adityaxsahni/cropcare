Summary

CropCare checks leaf photos on your phone. Photos are not saved and are never sent anywhere. Sharing anonymous results is optional and off until you say yes.

On your phone

  • Camera. The app asks for camera permission only when you open the camera screen. Each photo is checked on the phone and then discarded. It is not saved to your gallery or to the app.
  • History. The app keeps a list of your past checks on the phone: the condition found, how sure the app was, the time, and your Yes or No answer. This list never leaves the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Other permissions. The app uses the internet only for the optional sharing below and to download updated treatment advice. It does not ask for location, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Optional sharing

When you first open the app, it asks whether you want to help improve CropCare. If you say yes, then whenever the phone has internet the app sends, for each check:

  • the condition the app suggested and how sure it was
  • your answer to “Was this correct?”, if you gave one
  • the app and model version
  • the time of the check, rounded down to the hour
  • a random ID created by the app when you said yes, so we can count how many phones use the app. It is not linked to your phone number, your Google account or your device.

We never collect names, phone numbers, photos, location, contacts or device identifiers.

You can turn sharing off at any time on the app’s “About and privacy” screen. When you do, results waiting to be sent are deleted from the phone and the random ID is deleted. Results already sent cannot be linked back to you.

Treatment advice updates

When the phone is online, the app may check our server for a newer, expert-reviewed version of the treatment advice. This check sends no information about you or your checks.

Our server

Shared results are stored in a database used only for this project, to count users and checks and to measure how often farmers say the app was right. Our server keeps the IP address of each request in memory for about one minute to block floods of requests, and does not save it to the database. Our hosting provider may keep standard access logs as part of running the service.

We do not sell or share this data. We may publish totals, such as the number of checks or the share marked correct.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts.

Children

The app is meant for farmers and does not knowingly collect information from children.
