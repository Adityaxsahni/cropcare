**Pilot test build.** Free, for people helping test CropCare. Not ready for everyday farm use yet.

## Install on Android (7 or newer)

1. Download `cropcare-model-1.0.0.apk` below (about 75 MB). No account needed.
2. If an older CropCare is on the phone, uninstall it first (Settings > Apps > CropCare).
3. Open the downloaded file and allow installing from this source when Android asks.
4. Open CropCare and tap **Check a leaf**. Take the photo in the app, with the phone's own camera app, or pick one from the gallery.

On Realme, Oppo and Vivo phones, if it says "App not installed": allow the Files app under Settings > Privacy > Special app access > Install unknown apps, and pause Play Protect (Play Store > profile > Play Protect > settings) while installing.

## What to know

- Works without internet. Photos are checked on the phone and are not sent anywhere ([privacy policy](https://adityaxsahni.github.io/cropcare/privacy.html)).
- Model 1.0.0: on 89 real-world PlantDoc photos it is right 56% of the time, so it often says "not sure" (it answers about one photo in three). It has not been tested on our own field photos yet. All numbers: [results.md](https://github.com/Adityaxsahni/cropcare/blob/main/results.md).
- The treatment advice is a draft. An agriculture expert has not checked it yet; the app marks it as a draft.
- Signed with a test key, for sideloading only. It is not on the Play Store.
